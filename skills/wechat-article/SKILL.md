# WeChat Official Account Article (公众号文章)

A 公众号 article competes in a feed of headlines, and is read on a phone, often in a few minutes. The headline and digest decide the open; short paragraphs and clear sub-headings decide whether the reader finishes; the ending decides whether they share. This skill writes for that path and keeps the headline honest.

Write in Simplified Chinese.

## What This Skill Produces

- **Three headline options**, each a different angle
- **The digest (摘要)**: one line shown under the headline when shared
- **The article**: opening hook, sub-headed sections, short paragraphs, a strong close
- **Cover image guidance**: the main cover and the small square version
- **A call to action**: follow, share, comment or read more
- **A claim check**: every factual claim and its source

## Required Inputs

Ask for these if not provided:
- **The topic and the point**: what the reader should think or do after reading
- **The account and its readers**: who follows it, and the usual tone
- **Material**: notes, data, stories, quotes, with sources
- **Length**: short (about 1,500 characters) or long (3,000 or more)

## Framework

1. **Headline**: specific and true. Strong shapes: a surprising number, a question the reader has, a clear benefit, a story opening. Keep the main words in the first part, as long headlines are cut in some views.
2. **Digest**: one sentence, the article's promise, under about 50 characters.
3. **Opening**: a scene, a question or a striking fact, then why it matters to this reader, within the first screen.
4. **Body**: three to five sub-headed sections. Paragraphs of two to four lines on a phone. One idea per section. Bold the key sentence of a section sparingly.
5. **Close**: the takeaway in one or two sentences, then one call to action.
6. **Sources**: data and quotes cited in the text or at the end.

## Quality Checks
- [ ] Every headline matches what the article delivers
- [ ] The opening states why it matters within the first screen
- [ ] Paragraphs are short enough for a phone
- [ ] Every statistic or quote has a source
- [ ] There is exactly one main call to action

## Anti-Patterns
- **标题党 (clickbait).** It raises opens once and loses followers.
- **Walls of text.** On a phone, a long paragraph is skipped.
- **Unsourced statistics.**
- **Several calls to action** competing at the end.
