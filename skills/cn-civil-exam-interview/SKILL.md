# Civil Service Interview (结构化面试)

After the written exam, civil service and public institution candidates usually face a structured interview: a panel asks a fixed set of questions in a set time, and scores each candidate on the same dimensions. Good answers are organised, practical and suited to the post; weak ones are generic, run out of time, or recite templates the panel has heard many times. This skill rehearses the main question types with timed practice and specific feedback.

Write in Simplified Chinese. This is a study aid: formats vary by region and post, and the official interview announcement (面试公告) takes precedence.

## What This Skill Produces

- **A question-type guide** with what each type tests and a flexible answer structure
- **Worked examples** for each type, tailored to the post applied for
- **A timed mock round**: questions asked one at a time, with the time used tracked
- **Scored feedback** per answer on content, structure, post fit and delivery
- **A practice plan** to the interview date

## Required Inputs

Ask for these if not provided:
- **The post** (岗位) and the employing body: national ministry, local government, public institution
- **The interview format** in the announcement: number of questions, total time, whether questions are read aloud or given on paper, and whether there is a group discussion (无领导小组讨论)
- **Interview date** and how much practice time is available
- **Weak areas**, if the person knows them

## Framework

1. **Know the common types.**
   - 综合分析: a social phenomenon, policy or saying. Show a view, analyse causes and effects from several sides, and end with practical measures.
   - 计划组织协调: organise an activity, survey or campaign. Purpose, preparation, implementation with key details, and follow-up.
   - 人际关系: conflict with a colleague, leader or member of the public. Stay calm, take responsibility for your part, communicate, and put the work first.
   - 应急应变: an urgent incident. Safety first, control the situation, report upwards, solve, then review.
   - 自我认知与岗位匹配: why this post, and what you bring. Concrete experience linked to the post's duties.
   - 情景模拟: speak directly to the person in the scenario, in a suitable tone.
2. **Structure without sounding scripted.** A clear opening view, two to four organised points, and a short close. Vary wording; panels penalise stock phrases.
3. **Fit the post.** A tax bureau, a street office (街道) and a hospital ask different things of the same question type; examples should come from the post's real work.
4. **Manage time.** Divide the total time by the number of questions, keep a reserve, and practise to time.
5. **Feedback on four dimensions**: content (is it practical and specific), structure (is it clear), post fit, and delivery (pace, eye contact, filler words, from the person's own description or recording notes).

## Output Format

### 题型说明
| 题型 | 考查什么 | 答题思路 | 常见失分点 |

### 示范作答
[题目] → [示范答案，标注结构]

### 模拟面试
One question at a time. After each answer: 用时, then the feedback below.

### 点评
| 维度 | 评分（1 到 5） | 说明 | 改进建议 |
- 内容具体性
- 结构清晰度
- 岗位匹配度
- 表达（语速、停顿、口头禅）

### 备考计划
| 日期 | 练习内容 | 题量 | 复盘重点 |

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Every question type is explained with what it tests and a structure
- [ ] Worked examples use the person's post, not a generic one
- [ ] The mock round asks one question at a time and records time used
- [ ] Feedback scores all four dimensions with a specific improvement each
- [ ] Stock phrases in the person's answers are pointed out and replaced
- [ ] The guide notes that formats vary and the official announcement takes precedence

## Anti-Patterns

- **Memorised templates.** Panels hear the same openings all day and mark them down.
- **Grand statements with no action.** 综合分析 answers need practical measures.
- **Ignoring the post.** An answer that could be given for any job scores low on fit.
- **Overrunning on the first question.** It costs the last one.
