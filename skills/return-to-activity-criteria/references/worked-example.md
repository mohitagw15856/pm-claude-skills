# Worked Example — Return-to-Activity Criteria

## The brief

A 24-year-old semi-professional footballer, 26 weeks after ACL reconstruction (hamstring graft), wants to return for a promotion play-off in three weeks. The surgeon's protocol permits unrestricted running from week 16 and contact from week 24. Current measures: quadriceps limb symmetry index 84%, single-hop 91%, triple-hop 88%, isokinetic hamstring:quadriceps ratio 0.54 on the operated side, and a Y-balance anterior reach deficit of 5 cm. The player reports "feeling ready". The manager has asked twice when he is available. He has completed a graded running programme but no on-pitch contact work.

## The output

### Return-to-activity plan: [patient] · ACL reconstruction (hamstring graft, 26/52) · competitive football · 2026-08-25

**Target demand:** ~10 km per match with 20–30 maximal sprints, repeated cutting at 45–90°, single-leg landing from contested headers, unplanned deceleration under contact and fatigue in the final 15 minutes.
**Constraints:** surgeon's protocol — contact permitted from week 24 (met); no other restriction stated.
**Pressures:** named openly — play-off in 3 weeks, manager has asked twice, player self-reports readiness.

**Stages**

| Stage | Activity permitted | Criteria to progress | Tested how |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Linear running, gym strength | LSI ≥ 90% quads, pain-free | Isokinetic + hop battery |
| 2 | Planned change of direction | Hop battery ≥ 90% all four, H:Q ≥ 0.6 | Repeat battery |
| 3 | Unplanned COD, non-contact team drills | Stage 2 held **under fatigue** | Battery repeated post-conditioning |
| 4 | Full contact training | 2 weeks of stage 3 with no effusion or next-day soreness | Session load + clinical review |
| Return | Match minutes, graded | 3 full contact sessions completed | Clinical + coaching sign-off |

**Test battery**

| Test | Threshold | Under fatigue? | Result | Date | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quadriceps LSI (isokinetic 60°/s) | ≥ 90% | No | 84% | 25/08 | ✗ |
| Single-hop LSI | ≥ 90% | Yes | 91% | 25/08 | ✓ |
| Triple-hop LSI | ≥ 90% | Yes | 88% | 25/08 | ✗ |
| H:Q ratio, operated limb | ≥ 0.60 | No | 0.54 | 25/08 | ✗ |
| Y-balance anterior asymmetry | < 4 cm | No | 5 cm | 25/08 | ✗ |
| Contact sessions completed | ≥ 3 | — | 0 | — | ✗ |

**Graded reintroduction**

| Week | Volume | Intensity | Contact/load | Step back if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 sessions | Stage 3 drills | Non-contact | Effusion, or next-day soreness > 24h |
| 2 | 4 sessions | Add unplanned COD under fatigue | Non-contact | Any criterion regresses |
| 3 | Reassess battery | — | Contact if all pass | — |

**Residual risk discussed:** second-injury risk is meaningfully elevated in the first year after ACL reconstruction, and returning with criteria unmet raises it further. Meeting every criterion reduces risk; it does not remove it. Explained that a hamstring graft specifically affects the H:Q ratio, which is one of the failing measures. **Patient's understanding:** recorded — able to restate that four of six criteria are unmet and what each protects against.

**Decision: not cleared** for the play-off. Four of six criteria unmet, and zero contact sessions completed against a minimum of three — which alone makes the date unreachable, independent of strength.
**By:** treating clinician, with the club doctor informed.
**Not met, and the reasoning for not proceeding:** the deficits are in quadriceps strength and the hamstring:quadriceps ratio — the two measures most directly implicated in the mechanism this player would face.

**Monitoring after return:** session load, effusion, and next-morning soreness logged each session; battery repeated at 4 and 12 weeks post-return; return paused on any regression.

## What this example demonstrates

- **The demand is quantified**, so the criteria test the sport rather than a general notion of fitness.
- **Thresholds were set at week 2, not week 26.** They are being applied under exactly the pressure that bends criteria written in the moment.
- **"Feels ready" and 26 weeks are both visible and neither is a criterion.** Time-based clearance is the anti-pattern this whole document exists to prevent.
- **The unreachable item is named first.** Zero of three contact sessions makes the date impossible regardless of strength — which is a kinder and clearer conversation than debating a percentage.
- **The pressures are recorded in the document**, so the decision can be reviewed later on what was known, not on who was in the room.
