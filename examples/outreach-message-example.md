# Example: outreach-message

## What this skill does

Writes cold outreach and networking messages that get replies: a genuine hook for this specific recipient, one credibility line, a low-friction ask with an easy out, and a polite follow-up, tuned to the channel.

## How to trigger it

### Example 1: the referral request to an acquaintance

**You say:**
> "Message to Dani, a former colleague from two jobs ago, now at [company]. They just posted a senior data analyst role. We were friendly but haven't spoken in 3 years. I want a referral."

**Claude produces (excerpt):**

### Outreach: referral request → Dani (LinkedIn DM)

Hi Dani! It's been a while; I still quote your "every dashboard is a question someone stopped asking" line. Congrats on the move to [company].

I'm reaching out with a purpose, so I'll be upfront: the senior data analyst role your team posted looks like a genuine fit; I've spent the last two years doing exactly that churn-modelling work at [current company]. Would you be open to referring me, or to 10 minutes of honesty about whether I'd actually suit the team?

Totally fine if referrals feel awkward after three years of silence (that's on me); even a "here's what the hiring manager cares about" would be gold.

**A short follow-up (day 6):**
> Hi Dani, one nudge and then I'll leave it: the posting closes [date], so if a referral is doable this week it would mean a lot. Either way, good to be back in touch.

**Note on what makes it work:** it names the awkwardness (3 years, asking with a purpose) instead of performing casualness, which recipients see through; and it offers two sizes of yes.

### Example 2: the cold note to a hiring manager, no connection

**You say:**
> "Connection note (300 char limit) plus a fuller DM to the hiring manager for a product design role at [startup]. My hook: I redesigned onboarding at my current job and their public changelog shows they're mid-onboarding-revamp."

**Claude produces (excerpt):**

**Connection note (≤300 chars):**
> Hi [name], saw in your changelog you're rebuilding onboarding; I led exactly that at [company] (activation +18%). Applying for your product design role and wanted to reach you directly, not just the ATS. Happy to share what worked and what didn't.

**The fuller DM (after accept):** 4 sentences expanding the result, one line on why their approach in the changelog caught your eye (specific screen, not flattery), the ask ("worth 15 minutes before my application hits the pile?"), and the easy out.

## Tips for best results

- Give a real hook (their work, shared history, something they published); "I came across your profile" is deleted on sight.
- Say the relationship honestly, including lapsed ones; naming the gap works better than pretending it isn't there.
- Keep one ask per message; a referral and a coffee and feedback is three chances to say no.
