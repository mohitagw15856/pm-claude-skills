# Example: creator-brand-kit

## What this skill does

Defines a creator's brand foundation on one page: niche, audience, positioning, content pillars, voice with do/don't examples, and bios, reusable by the other content skills so everything sounds like you.

## How to trigger it

### Example 1: a brand kit from scratch

**You say:**
> "Build my brand kit. I'm a payroll accountant making TikToks about money mistakes small businesses make. Audience: UK founders under 10 staff. I'm blunt but kind; I never do finance-bro content."

**Claude produces (excerpt):**

### 1. Niche & positioning
- **Niche:** UK small-business founders + payroll and money operations + the mistakes an accountant actually sees
- **Positioning line:** "I help founders under 10 staff stop bleeding money on payroll and tax mistakes, using real cases from my desk."
- **What makes you different:** practising accountant with case receipts, not a course-seller.

### 3. Content pillars
1. **Mistake of the week** (grow): a real anonymised payroll error and its cost, e.g. "[Founder] paid £4,200 in penalties for one missed pension re-enrolment."
2. **Do this before the deadline** (nurture): calendar-pegged actions, VAT, year-end, P11D.
3. **Ask the accountant** (convert): answering comments, ending with the consultation link.

### 4. Voice & tone
- **Blunt:** do: "This mistake costs £800. Stop making it." don't: "You might perhaps want to consider..."
- **Kind:** do: "Everyone does this once. Here's the fix." don't: mockery of any founder.
- **Words to avoid:** hack, passive income, game-changer.

### 5. Bio
"Payroll accountant. I post the mistakes before you make them. UK small business. [link]" (94 chars)

### Example 2: tightening an existing brand

**You say:**
> "I have 12k followers but my last 20 posts are all over the place: productivity, AI tools, one about my dog. Rebuild the pillars around what grew: the AI-tools posts got 80% of saves."

**Claude produces (excerpt):**

**Niche sharpened:** AI tools + [your profession] workflows + tested-by-me verdicts.

**Pillars rebuilt:** 1) Tool tested this week (grow), 2) My real workflow (nurture), 3) What I would not pay for (trust, and your differentiator: a negative-verdict pillar almost nobody runs).

**Reuse note:** paste this kit into content-repurposer and hook-writer so every post inherits the verdict voice; retire the off-pillar topics to a monthly "office hours" post rather than deleting them cold.

## Tips for best results

- Name the audience by situation, not demographics: "founders under 10 staff" beats "25 to 40 year olds".
- Bring your best and worst performing posts; pillars should follow evidence.
- Write the don't examples honestly; voice is defined by what you refuse to sound like.

## Related skills

- `content-repurposer` to produce the weekly pack in this kit's voice
- `hook-writer` when the pillar is right but the first line keeps failing
