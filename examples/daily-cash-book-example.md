# Example: Daily Cash Book

## What this skill does
Sets up the simplest daily money record that shows whether a small business makes money, and keeps business and household money apart.

## How to trigger it

### Example 1: A small shop
**You say:**
> "I run a small grocery shop. I sell about 800 a day but at the end of the month there is nothing left."

**Claude produces:**
**1. Your daily page**
| Date | Money in | Money out (stock / transport / other) | Cash at end of day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon 6 | 820 | 500 stock, 40 transport | 280 |

**2. Three rules**
1. Keep business cash in one place, separate from household money.
2. Pay the household a fixed amount from the business each week, for example 600, and write it down.
3. Write the page at the same time every day: two minutes.

**Why there is nothing left:** if household spending also comes out of the shop's cash, the shop looks unprofitable even when it is not. The weekly summary will show which.

---

### Example 2: Records for a loan
**You say:**
> "The microfinance officer wants my business records and I have none."

**Claude produces:**
Start the daily page today. Lenders usually want to see a steady pattern; three months of daily records plus weekly totals is a strong start. Bring the book and the weekly summaries to the meeting.

---

## Tips for best results
- Say what you sell and whether you buy stock to resell
- Give rough daily sales and costs if you know them
- Say whether you prefer paper or your phone
