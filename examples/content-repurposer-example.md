# Example: content-repurposer

## What this skill does

Turns one piece of content into a platform-native pack: an X thread, LinkedIn post, newsletter section, carousel and a short-form video script, each rewritten for its platform rather than cross-posted.

## How to trigger it

### Example 1: a blog post into a full pack

**You say:**
> "Repurpose my post 'Why we killed our free tier' [pasted]. Key points: support load from free users was 61% of tickets, conversion to paid was 0.9%, revenue grew 18% within two months of removal."

**Claude produces (excerpt):**

**The core idea:** a free tier that does not convert is not marketing, it is an unpaid support contract.

### 🧵 X/Twitter thread
1/ We killed our free tier. Revenue went up 18% in two months. Here's the maths nobody shows you:
2/ Free users were 61% of our support tickets and 0.9% of conversions. Every "free" account cost real money.
3/ The fear: the funnel dies. The reality: trials with a card converted 11x better.
[... beats 4 to 7 ...]
8/ If your free tier converts under 2%, you do not have a funnel. You have a queue. Full numbers: [link]

### 💼 LinkedIn post
We made a decision most SaaS founders are scared of.

We deleted our free tier.

Not because free users were bad people. Because the unit maths was a slow leak: 61% of support, 0.9% of conversions.
[... whitespace body and soft question CTA ...]

### 🎬 Short-form video script
0-3s hook: "Deleting our free tier made us 18% more money."
[on-screen: "0.9% conversion"] Body beats and payoff follow.

**Posting order & cadence:** thread day 1, LinkedIn day 2 morning, newsletter day 3, carousel day 5, video day 7.

### 📧 Newsletter section
Subject option: "Send it when they're on the sofa". 180-word section with the one chart to include and a caveat: measured on B2C, test before copying.

## Tips for best results

- Paste the source content and the two or three numbers that carry it; hooks are built from specifics.
- Say which platforms you actually post on; skip the rest.
- Give your voice in one line ("dry, no emojis") or attach your brand kit so the pack sounds like you.
