# Example: where-do-i-start

## What this skill does

Turns an overwhelming brain-dump into one clear first action: your pile captured as a list, ordered by one simple logic, exactly one next physical step revealed, and everything else deliberately parked.

## How to trigger it

### Example 1: the everything-at-once pile

**You say:**
> "I don't know where to start. In my head: tax return due end of month, inbox at 300+, flat viewing to book, boss wants the Q4 plan draft, gym membership to cancel, mum's birthday present, and I think my passport expires soon??"

**Claude produces (excerpt):**

### Your pile (captured):
1. Tax return (deadline: end of month)
2. Q4 plan draft (boss waiting)
3. Passport expiry (unknown date, possible hard deadline)
4. Flat viewing to book
5. Mum's birthday present
6. Gym membership cancellation
7. Inbox at 300+

**Ordered by:** hard deadlines and unknowns-that-could-be-deadlines first, then what unblocks someone else, then everything that will still be fine next week.

### 👉 Start here. The only thing right now:
**Get your passport, look at the expiry date, and put it back.** Takes ~2 minutes.

It is the only item where you don't know if it's urgent, and not knowing is what makes the whole pile hum. Two minutes converts it from a background dread into either a task with a date or nothing at all.

*(Everything else is parked. The inbox is explicitly not your problem today; 300 unread is a number, not a deadline. Come back and say "done" and I'll give you the next one, one at a time.)*

### Example 2: coming back for the next one

**You say:**
> "Done. Expires in 5 weeks, and I need it for a work trip in 7."

**Claude produces (excerpt):**

Good catch; that was nearly an emergency. It is now simply first.

### 👉 The only thing right now:
**Open the passport renewal page and start the application.** Takes ~15 minutes with a photo; if you don't have a digital photo, the task shrinks to "find the nearest photo booth or pharmacy that does them" and that alone counts as done.

*(The tax return is next after this, but it does not exist until you say "done".)*

## Tips for best results

- Dump everything, half-formed included; an unlisted pile is what overwhelms, a listed one is just a list.
- Trust the one-at-a-time reveal; asking to see the whole ordered list re-creates the overwhelm you came in with.
- Say "done" even for partial progress; momentum is the mechanism, not completeness.
