# Example: overwhelm-triage

## What this skill does

Sorts an everything-is-urgent pile into four buckets fast: do now, schedule, drop, delegate, with the honest drop calls most people won't make themselves and one thing to do right now.

## How to trigger it

### Example 1: the drowning week

**You say:**
> "Everything is urgent and I can't tell what matters: client deliverable Friday, teammate's PR waiting on my review 2 days, performance reviews due next week, conference talk I said I'd submit (closes tonight), dentist to rebook, 40 unread Slack threads, mum asked me to call, and the team offsite needs a venue by ???"

**Claude produces (excerpt):**

### The pile: 8 items, captured.

**🔴 Do now** (just 2):
- Conference talk: the only true today-deadline in the pile. But see the drop test below first.
- Teammate's PR: 2 days blocked is you being someone else's overwhelm; 30 minutes clears it.

**🗓 Schedule** (real, but not today):
- Client deliverable → tomorrow morning, first 3 hours, calendar-blocked (Friday is real; today is not its day)
- Performance reviews → Thursday afternoon
- Call mum → tonight's walk home (this one is load-bearing for you, not just her)

**🗑 Drop** (feels required, isn't):
- The conference talk, probably. Test: if submitting closed last night, would you feel relief or loss? If relief, that answer was available all along; let it close. There is another deadline next quarter.
- 38 of the 40 Slack threads. Unread is not a debt. Skim for your name, mark the rest read without ceremony.

**🤝 Delegate / ask for help:**
- Offsite venue → ask the teammate whose PR you're about to unblock; "venue by Friday, budget X" is a complete handover. The "by ???" means it was never urgent, just loud.
- Dentist → 2 minutes, but it can also be Saturday's problem.

**👉 Right now, just:** decide the talk (relief-or-loss test, 1 minute), then review the PR.
**The truth:** not all of this has to happen today. Of eight items, today actually owns two.

### Example 2: when the urgency is someone else's

**You say:**
> "My manager marks everything urgent. Today: 'urgent' deck feedback, 'urgent' numbers pull, 'urgent' intro email. I also have my actual project due."

**Claude produces (excerpt):**

**Challenge the urgency:** three "urgents" from one person is a labelling habit, not a fact about the world. Test each against real consequence: the deck is presented *Thursday* (schedule), the intro email has no date at all (schedule), the numbers feed a meeting *today at 4* (genuinely do-now).

**👉 Right now, just:** the numbers pull. Then send one message: "Numbers by 2pm. Deck feedback tomorrow AM, intro Wednesday; shout if that breaks anything." You've triaged their urgency for them, visibly, once; that message trains the next week.

## Tips for best results

- Dump everything, including the personal items; the mix is what makes the swirl.
- Let the drop bucket be uncomfortable; the calls that sting slightly are the ones doing the work.
- Take the one do-now action before re-reading the list; the list is the trap.
