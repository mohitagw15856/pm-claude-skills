# Example: Newcomer School Enrolment

## What this skill does
Turns "we just moved, how do we get our child into school?" into an ordered plan, a document checklist with fallbacks, and the questions to ask.

## How to trigger it

### Example 1: Missing birth certificate
**You say:**
> "We moved to a new city last week. My son is 9 and finished grade 3 at home. We lost his birth certificate in the move. How do I enrol him?"

**Claude produces:**
**1. Your enrolment plan**
1. Contact the local education authority's school admissions team (search the city council website for "school admissions"). They allocate places, not individual schools *(assumed, confirm)*.
2. Ask for an in-year application, since the school year has started.
3. While you wait, keep a dated note of every call and email.

**2. Documents to bring**
| Document | Have it? | If not |
|---|---|---|
| Birth certificate | No | Passport, ID card or a sworn statement of date of birth; ask the authority which they accept |
| Proof of address | ? | Tenancy agreement, a utility bill or a letter from your landlord |
| Vaccination record | ? | Book a catch-up appointment; it should not delay enrolment |

---

### Example 2: Language support
**You say:**
> "My daughter is 12 and does not speak the language yet. Will she be put in a lower year?"

**Claude produces:**
In most school systems, children are placed by age, not by language level, and language support is given alongside normal lessons. Ask: "How do you assess new pupils who are learning the language, and what support is available in class?" Holding a child back a year for language alone usually slows their progress with friends and lessons.

---

## Tips for best results
- Say which country and city you live in now
- List the documents you have and the ones you are missing
- Mention any learning or health needs the school should know about
