# Example: investor-cold-email

## What this skill does

Writes investor outreach that gets replies: a sub-120-word cold email with the best traction number up front and a specific ask, a forwardable blurb a mutual contact can paste unedited, and a follow-up that adds news rather than "bumping".

## How to trigger it

### Example 1: the cold email with real traction

**You say:**
> "Email to Sarah Chen at [fund], she writes about vertical SaaS and backed two logistics companies. We're Freightline: £41k MRR, 19% MoM for 5 months, 2 of the top 20 UK hauliers signed. Raising £1.5m seed."

**Claude produces (excerpt):**

**Subject:** Freightline: £41k MRR, +19% MoM, raising seed

Hi Sarah,

I'm [name], co-founder of Freightline; we're at £41k MRR growing 19% month-on-month for five straight months.

We're the operating system for mid-size hauliers: dispatch, proof-of-delivery and invoicing in one place, replacing the fax-and-spreadsheet stack. The timing driver is the e-invoicing mandate landing in 2027; every haulier has to digitise something next year, and we're what they digitise into.

Two of the UK's twenty largest hauliers signed in the last quarter, both from inbound referral.

Your pieces on vertical SaaS workflow lock-in, and the [portfolio company] investment, suggest this is squarely your thesis. Raising £1.5m; open to a 20-minute call in the next two weeks? Deck attached.

[name]

### Example 2: the forwardable blurb and the follow-up

**You say:**
> "A founder she backed offered to intro us. Give me the blurb he can forward, and the follow-up if she goes quiet."

**Claude produces (excerpt):**

**Forwardable blurb (for him to paste as-is):**

Sarah, intro-ing [name], co-founder of Freightline: vertical SaaS for mid-size hauliers (dispatch, POD, invoicing). £41k MRR, 19% MoM for five months, two top-20 UK hauliers signed, both inbound. Feels like your workflow lock-in thesis a year early. Raising £1.5m seed; worth 20 minutes. I'll let [name] take it from here if you're interested.

**The follow-up (day 6, only with news):**

Hi Sarah, quick update since my last note: [major haulier] went live this week, taking us past £48k MRR. The round now has a lead circling, so timing has tightened; still keen to include you. 20 minutes this week?

**Why these work:** the blurb makes the forwarder look good (he's surfacing signal, not asking a favour), and the follow-up earns its place with two new facts instead of "just bumping this".

## Tips for best results

- Lead with your single best traction number; it is the subject line, not the appendix.
- Give one genuine reason for *this* investor; thesis fit in one line beats three lines of flattery.
- Have real news before following up; a milestone-free nudge spends credibility to say nothing.
