# Example: Crop Insurance Claim

## What this skill does
Works out the type of crop policy, the deadline, the evidence needed, and how to follow up or appeal.

## How to trigger it

### Example 1: Drought loss
**You say:**
> "Drought destroyed half my maize. I got insurance through my bank loan. How do I claim?"

**Claude produces:**
**1. Your claim timeline**
1. **Today:** ask the bank which insurer covers the loan and what the reporting deadline is. Many policies need the loss reported within days, so report first and gather evidence after.
2. **Today or tomorrow:** take dated photos of the field from several angles with a landmark in view. Do not clear or replant yet.
3. **This week:** send the written notice below to the bank and the insurer, and keep a copy.

**Note:** insurance taken with a bank loan is often an area or weather-index policy. That means the payout depends on rainfall or area yield data, not only on your field. Ask which kind you have.

---

### Example 2: Claim refused
**You say:**
> "They rejected my claim saying rainfall was normal at the station."

**Claude produces:**
That sounds like a weather-index policy. You can usually challenge whether the right weather station and dates were used, not your farm's own loss. Ask in writing for: the station used, the rainfall figures, and the trigger level. If the nearest station is far from your farm and recorded different rainfall from local records, raise that in a written complaint, then with the regulator or ombudsman if there is one.

---

## Tips for best results
- Say how you were enrolled (bank loan, cooperative, app, directly)
- Give the date the loss was noticed and what caused it
- Keep and mention any reply from the insurer
