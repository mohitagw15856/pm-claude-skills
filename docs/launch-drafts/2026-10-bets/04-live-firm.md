## Headline
I let an AI executive team run my open-source repo. Its predictions are scored automatically.

## Post
> Each Monday, five AI roles meet on the repository's live numbers (stars, npm downloads, open issues, recent pull requests), argue, decide, and commit to one prediction a script can check, like "stars at least 1,600 by 30 November". When the date comes, the prediction is scored against the real number and the result cannot be edited. The track record is public.
