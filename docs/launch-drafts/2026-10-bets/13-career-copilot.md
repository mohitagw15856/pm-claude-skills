**Status:** Live now.

## One line
Save your CV once, track applications, and get the next step for each: tailor, research, follow up, prepare, negotiate.

## Headline
A private job-search copilot: your next step for every application, with your CV already filled in

## Post
> Paste your CV once and add the jobs you are going for. For each one it works out the next step from where it stands and how long it has been there, and opens the right skill with your CV and that job already in. Everything stays in your browser.
