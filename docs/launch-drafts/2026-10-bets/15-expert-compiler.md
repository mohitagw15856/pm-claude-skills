**Status:** Live now.

## One line
An interview for professionals that compiles their answers into a standards-compliant AI skill, credited to them.

## Headline
Turn your professional know-how into an open AI skill, built from your answers rather than a model's guesses

## Post
> Answer questions about one situation you handle well: what you need to know, your steps, rules of thumb, red flags, the mistakes people make, when to send someone to a professional, and one real case. It compiles your answers into a SkillSpec skill with an eval case and your credit line, ready for a pull request. No model needed.
