## One line
Every time the library rewrote one of its own skills: the score before, the score after, and whether it was kept. Losses included.

## Headline
An open-source library that rewrites its weakest skills and logs every attempt, including the failures

## Post
> Three loops rewrite the lowest-scoring skills; a judge compares each rewrite with the original and only a clear win is kept, and a person still reviews the pull request. The evolution log records every attempt, so you can check the claim line by line.
