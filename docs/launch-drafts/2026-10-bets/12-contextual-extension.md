# The browser extension that knows which skill you need

**Status:** After the owner publishes 1.2.0 to the Chrome, Firefox and Edge stores.
**Shipped in:** [#360](https://github.com/mohitagw15856/pm-claude-skills/pull/360)

## One line
On a lease, a job ad or a medical bill, the extension suggests the matching skill. Matching happens on your device.

## Headline
A browser extension that suggests the right expert checklist for the page you are on, without sending the page anywhere

## Post
> Click the toolbar button on a lease, a job posting or a medical bill and it suggests the skills for that situation. Opt in and it does it as you browse, one dismissible card per page, never on sign-in or banking pages. Nothing about the page leaves your browser.
