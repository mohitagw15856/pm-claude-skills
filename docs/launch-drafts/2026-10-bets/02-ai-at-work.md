## Post
> Benchmarks rank models on maths and trivia. This one runs the same real tasks every month (PRDs, post-mortems, performance reviews, pricing) on each model, twice: on its own and with a professional skill. A pinned judge scores both. Results are grouped by job, gaps under 0.25 are called too close, and stale runs are marked. Everything is open, including the negative findings.
