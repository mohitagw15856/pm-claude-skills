## Headline
A live badge that grades your repo's agent skills (SkillSpec L1 to L3)

## Post
> Paste one line in your README and the badge grades the skills your repo ships: loadable, structured, or trustworthy (inputs, output, quality checks, anti-patterns). It reports the weakest skill, because one bad skill is the one your agent loads.
