# Pitch templates

Short first emails to a possible sponsor. Fill the square brackets. Take the figures from the "Who you reach" table in [SPONSORS.md](../../SPONSORS.md) on the day you send, and do not round them up.

## First contact

**Subject:** A sponsor slot in front of [N] product people using AI assistants

Hi [name],

I maintain PM Skills, an open-source library of professional skills for AI assistants: https://github.com/mohitagw15856/pm-claude-skills

I am writing because [their product] is something our readers reach for straight after using [skill name]: [one sentence on the link between the skill and their product].

The numbers, as of [date]: [stars] GitHub stars, [views] repository views and [uniques] unique visitors in the last 14 days, and [npm] npm downloads in the last 30 days. Most visitors arrive from search.

The README banner is open for [month]. It sits directly under the title, is exclusive for the month, and comes with a report of views and referrers at the end of the term. The full list of placements and terms is here: https://github.com/mohitagw15856/pm-claude-skills/blob/main/SPONSORS.md

Would a month be worth a look? I can send pricing and the banner spec.

Mohit

## If they ask what they get

- One sponsor per slot for the whole term, labelled "Sponsored by".
- Light and dark banners at 1200 by 300, with your UTM link.
- A report within five days of the term ending: repository views, front-page views, referrers and npm downloads. Clicks show in your own analytics under your UTM campaign.
- First refusal on the next term until ten days before yours ends.

## If they want a sponsored skill

- It is a skill about doing a job with your own product, labelled as sponsored in its description and first line.
- It goes through the same checks as every other skill and can be declined.
- It does not compare you with competitors, and sponsorship does not change eval scores or rankings.
- You supply the product documentation and one reviewer.

## Follow-up after a week

**Subject:** Re: A sponsor slot in front of [N] product people

Hi [name], a short follow-up. The [month] banner is still open and I would rather give it to a product our readers already use. If it is not for you, no reply needed, and if someone else owns sponsorships I would be glad of a pointer.

Mohit
