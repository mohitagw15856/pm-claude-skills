One new skill this week — and it pays for itself the first time you use it.

💸 Lower My Bill

Providers bank on you not calling. The loyal customer on the old plan quietly pays the "just renewed at full price" tax while new customers get the deal. Lower My Bill flips that around.

Give it your provider, what you pay now, and any leverage you have — it hands back:

  • A ready-to-read script (call or chat) in words you would actually say
  • The specific leverage that moves your price — a competitor quote, loyalty length, or the size of the increase
  • A fallback ladder for when they say no: partial discount → downgrade → the retention-desk "cancel" lever → walk-away math
  • The make-it-stick note — name, date, promised price, confirmation number — so the deal does not quietly revert next month

It is not a scam or a threat. It is asking, with leverage, the way the pricing is designed to be asked.

Try it

Just say: "My internet bill jumped from £35 to £52 — help me get it back down."
