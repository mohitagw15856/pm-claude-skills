# The life-expansion wave — 5 new bundles, 50 skills

_New release · v74.0.0 · now 1058 skills_

The library grows past the office. Five new bundles — 50 skills — for the parts of life that matter just as much as work: learning, money, relationships, caring for aging parents, and using AI itself well.

## 📖 pm-learning — learn anything and make it stick
Roadmaps, the Feynman technique, spaced repetition, plateau-breakers, deliberate practice, and note systems that actually retain.

## 💰 pm-wealth — build wealth on purpose (educational, not financial advice)
Investing for beginners, index funds, your first $100k, asking for a raise, compound growth, and a financial-independence roadmap — jurisdiction-neutral, always "not advice."

## 🤝 pm-social — the human conversations
Make friends as an adult, network as an introvert, set boundaries, give hard feedback kindly, de-escalate conflict, and repair after a fight.

## 🩺 pm-caregiving — care for aging parents (not medical/legal/financial advice)
Advocate at appointments, coordinate the care team, manage medications, weigh long-term-care options, check for caregiver burnout, and hold the end-of-life-wishes conversation.

## 🤖 pm-ai-native — use AI itself well
Build a prompt library, delegate to AI like a capable new hire, prime it with context, design AI workflows, verify its output, spot its mistakes, and get dramatically more from every tool.
