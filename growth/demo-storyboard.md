# Demo video storyboard — 60 seconds that convert

One video, reused everywhere (README, Show HN first comment, Product Hunt gallery, newsletters, socials). Goal: a viewer feels the *wow* of a real problem → pro-grade output in under a minute.

## The shot list (≈60s)
1. **0–5s — the hook.** Black screen, one line of text: *"Your AI gives you filler. Watch it give you a professional's answer instead."*
2. **5–15s — the problem.** Screen-record the [Playground](https://mohitagw15856.github.io/pm-claude-skills/). Type a real, relatable problem into `find`: *"my landlord is keeping my deposit."* It names the skill.
3. **15–40s — the payoff.** Click into [security-deposit-recovery](../skills/security-deposit-recovery/SKILL.md), fill the short form, hit run. Let the structured output **stream** — the severity scale, the demand-letter template, the money math. This is the money shot; don't rush it.
4. **40–52s — the range.** Fast cuts (1s each) of three wildly different skills' outputs: a PRD, a eulogy, a debt-collector script. *"1,117 of them. Work and life."*
5. **52–60s — the CTA.** *"Free in your browser. Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor. ⭐ on GitHub."* + the URL.

## Production notes
- **No talking head needed.** Screen recording + text overlays + soft music converts fine and is fast to make.
- **Record at 1080p+, export a GIF** (first 15s) for the README and a full MP4 for PH/YouTube.
- **Caption everything** — most social views are muted.
- **The streaming output is the star.** If the tool streams, capture it in real time; the "senior artifact appearing" moment is the whole pitch.
