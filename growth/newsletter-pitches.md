# AI-newsletter pitches — lead with the story, not the count

Newsletters (TLDR AI, Ben's Bites, The Rundown, Superhuman, Last Week in AI) run **stories**, not catalogues. "1,117 skills" is a stat; "someone built an AI toolkit for life after incarceration, grief, and debt collectors" is a story they'll run. Pitch the **dignity angle**.

## Subject lines (pick per outlet)
- An open-source AI toolkit for life's hardest paperwork — grief, layoffs, reentry
- The 1,100-skill library where the *life* skills beat the *work* skills
- What 1,100 hand-written AI skills reveal about where LLMs fail

## The pitch (short — they're busy)
> Hi [name],
>
> Quick one for [newsletter]. I maintain **PM Skills** — an open-source, MIT-licensed library of 1,117 "Agent Skills" (plain-markdown files that teach Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini to do one real task to a professional's standard). It's in Anthropic's official plugin directory and every skill runs free in the browser.
>
> The angle I think your readers would like: **the most-used skills aren't the work ones.** The lease decoder, a "money in crisis" triage, a grief-logistics checklist, and a just-built *reentry* bundle (life after incarceration) get more use than any PRD template. It's a small case study in AI meeting people at their hardest moments — and in where generic LLM answers fail real people.
>
> Playground (try any skill, no signup): [link]
> Repo: [link]
>
> Happy to write a short guest blurb or pull the numbers if useful. Either way, love your work.
> — [name]

## Targeting notes
- **TLDR AI / The Rundown:** lead with the stat + the "where LLMs fail" hook (dev-leaning audience).
- **Ben's Bites:** lead with the human/dignity story (loves a builder narrative).
- **Newsletters with a "cool tool" slot:** pitch the Playground as the tool, one line.
- **Always include the demo GIF** and offer a ready-made 2–3 sentence blurb so they can copy-paste.
