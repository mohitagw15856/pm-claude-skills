## PR etiquette (so they actually merge)
- **One list = one focused PR.** Match their exact formatting and alphabetization.
- **Put it in the *right* category** — don't jam it under a generic heading.
- **Read CONTRIBUTING.** Some require a screenshot, a certain description length, or no marketing adjectives — strip "amazing/powerful."
- **Keep the description factual and short.** Maintainers reject hype.
- **Track them** in a simple checklist; follow up politely if stale after a week.

## Bonus: get *others* to list you
Once the demo video and a Show HN/PH launch exist, list-maintainers add you unprompted. The awesome-list PRs and the launch reinforce each other.
