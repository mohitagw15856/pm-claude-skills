## In India
- There is no single national deposit-protection law. What applies depends on the state's rent law and, above all, the written rent agreement: check its deposit, notice and deduction clauses first.
- The Model Tenancy Act 2021 is a template that states may adopt. Where a state has adopted it, the residential deposit is capped at two months' rent, and the deposit must be refunded when the tenant hands over vacant possession, after lawful deductions. Check whether your state has adopted it before relying on this.
- Keep the agreement, rent receipts, move-in photos and a written handover note. Send the demand for refund in writing (email or registered post) with a clear deadline.
- If the landlord does not respond, the options are the Rent Authority or Rent Court where the state has set one up, otherwise a civil court. A legal notice from a lawyer often resolves it first.
