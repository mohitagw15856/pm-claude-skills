## In California (residential tenancies)
- Under Civil Code section 1950.5, the landlord must return the deposit, or send an itemised statement of deductions with the balance, within 21 days after the tenant moves out. Deductions over $125 need copies of receipts or invoices.
- Deductions are limited to unpaid rent, cleaning to the condition at move-in, repair of damage beyond ordinary wear and tear, and items the lease allows. Normal wear and tear cannot be charged.
- The tenant may ask for an initial inspection before moving out, so they can fix listed problems and avoid deductions.
- If a deposit is kept in bad faith, a court may award up to twice the deposit as a penalty, on top of the amount owed. Small claims court handles these disputes without a lawyer.
