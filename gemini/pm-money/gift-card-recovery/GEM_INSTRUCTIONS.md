# Gift-Card Recovery

Billions sit unspent in gift cards and store credit — forgotten in drawers and apps, slowly draining to fees or expiry. This helps you find what you're owed, understand the expiry and fee rules where you live, and actually use, convert, or (where allowed) cash out those balances before they vanish.

## What This Skill Produces

- **A find-and-check plan** — where to look for cards/credit and how to check each balance
- **The rules for your region** — expiry limits, dormancy fees, and any right to cash out small balances
- **Use-it options** — best ways to spend it, combine partials, or convert to something you'll actually use
- **Resale/convert routes** — reputable ways to trade unwanted cards for cash or other cards, with the discount to expect
- **Scam avoidance** — the gift-card scams to steer clear of (both losing value and being used as a scam payment)

## Required Inputs

Ask for these if not provided:
- **What you have** — the retailers/brands, rough balances, physical or digital
- **Card type** — store-specific or open-loop (Visa/Mastercard prepaid)
- **Region** — determines expiry/fee/cash-out rules
- **Your goal** — use it up, convert to cash, or just check it's still valid
- **Any problem** — lost card, drained balance, or a card someone pressured you to buy

## Framework: Find It, Know The Rules, Use Or Convert

1. **Locate and check.** Round up physical and digital cards and check each balance (retailer site/app/number) — you can't use what you've forgotten.
2. **Know expiry and fees.** Many regions limit how fast gift cards can expire and cap dormancy fees; some require cashing out small balances. Know your rights before assuming it's dead.
3. **Use it well.** Spend on something you'd buy anyway, stack partial balances, or apply store credit to a needed purchase — avoid buying junk just to "use it up."
4. **Convert unwanted cards.** Reputable gift-card exchanges buy/sell at a discount; know the haircut and stick to trustworthy platforms.
5. **Avoid the scams.** Never pay a "bill," "fine," or "fee" with gift cards (a hallmark scam), and beware balance-draining fraud on cards bought from open racks.

## Anti-Patterns
- **Assuming a card is worthless/expired** without checking the rules.
- **Buying random junk** just to use up a balance.
- **Using a sketchy exchange** that never pays.
- **Ignoring dormancy fees** quietly eating the balance.
- **Missing the scam angle** — gift cards as fake "payment."
