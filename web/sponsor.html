Terms

  • Fixed term. Agreed up front, normally one month from the day the placement goes live.
  • Exclusive. One sponsor per placement for the term.
  • Renewal. First refusal on the next term until ten days before yours ends.
  • Report. Within five days of the term ending: repository views, views of the page your placement sits on, referrers and npm downloads. Clicks are counted on your side under your UTM campaign, since GitHub does not report outbound clicks.
  • Links. UTM parameters are welcome. Scripts and tracking pixels are not accepted.

What sponsorship does not buy

  • Eval scores, benchmark results or leaderboard position.
  • A mention inside a skill that is not labelled as sponsored.
  • A comparison with competitors: a sponsored skill covers your own product only, and passes the same checks as every other skill.
