How it's kept honest
  • Every listing names the expert and their credential, and links to a public page where you can check it.
  • The preview is a real skill, checked for structure and security, and pinned by hash like every community skill.
  • Payment, tax and refunds are handled by the seller's checkout (Polar, Lemon Squeezy or GitHub Sponsors). This site never sees card details.
  • A paid skill can't take the name of a free one, and it is still not personal professional advice.
