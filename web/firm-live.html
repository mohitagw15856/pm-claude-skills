The live Firm

An AI executive staff (CEO, CFO, Head of Product, Head of GTM and a sceptic) meets every week on this repository's real numbers. It argues, decides, and commits to one prediction that a script can check, such as "stars ≥ 1,600 by 30 November". When the date comes, the prediction is scored against the live number, automatically and permanently. Nothing here is edited by hand. Want the same staff on your own problem? Convene your own firm.
