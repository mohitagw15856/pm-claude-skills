# 留学申请文书

Applications from China are often rejected for sounding alike: a list of grades and competitions, a quotation, a childhood dream, and conclusions about being "hardworking and passionate". Admissions readers want one person's thinking, shown through specific experience. This skill finds the material, chooses what to say, and coaches the English. It works from what the applicant actually did and keeps the writing theirs.

Coach in Simplified Chinese. Write the essay text in English.

## What This Skill Produces

- An inventory of the applicant's material: courses, projects, research, work, activities, setbacks
- A positioning line: who this applicant is for this programme
- An outline for each document required
- Feedback on a draft against the common faults
- An English draft or revision built from the applicant's own material
- A CV structure and a briefing note for recommenders
- An integrity note on originality and the institution's rules on outside help and AI

## Required Inputs

Ask for these if not provided:

- Level and country: undergraduate, master's or doctorate; UK, US, Canada, Australia, Hong Kong, Singapore, Europe
- Programmes and the exact prompts, with word or character limits
- Academic record, tests, and the subject applied for
- Experiences: what the applicant did, their role, what was hard, what changed
- Why this subject and why these programmes, in the applicant's own words (Chinese is fine)
- Career or research plans
- Any draft so far
- The schools' stated policy on AI and third-party help, if known

## Framework

1. **Know the document.**
   - **Master's SOP or PS:** academic motivation, preparation, fit with the programme, plans. Forward-looking and specific to the course.
   - **Doctoral statement:** research interests, experience, proposed direction, fit with named faculty.
   - **UK undergraduate:** the statement is answered as structured questions on why the course, how studies have prepared the applicant, and what has been done outside formal education. About the subject, not the person's whole life.
   - **US undergraduate:** a personal essay about the person, plus short supplements on why this college. Voice and reflection matter more than achievements.
2. **Inventory before writing.** For each experience: what I did, the difficulty, the decision I made, the result, what I think differently now.
3. **Choose two or three experiences** that show the same quality from different angles. Leave the rest for the CV.
4. **Show, then reflect.** A concrete scene or problem, then what the applicant learned and how it leads to the programme.
5. **Fit is specific.** Named modules, methods, labs or faculty, tied to something the applicant has already done.
6. **Common faults in Chinese applications.**
   - Listing achievements without the applicant's own role or thinking
   - Opening with a quotation, a childhood story or "ever since I was young"
   - Abstract virtues: hardworking, passionate, responsible
   - Translating Chinese rhetorical structure, with the point arriving in the last paragraph
   - Praising the university's ranking instead of explaining fit
   - The same essay sent to every programme
   - Template phrases from agency samples
7. **Language.** Plain, precise English. Short sentences. The applicant's level of English should be believable against their test scores.
8. **Recommenders.** Give each a one-page brief: what to cover, specific incidents, the programmes and deadlines. The recommender writes the letter.
9. **Integrity.** The content must be true and the applicant must be able to discuss every sentence at interview. Many institutions restrict third-party writing and AI-generated text; follow the stated policy and disclose where required.

## Quality Checks

- [ ] The document type and prompt are matched exactly, including the limit
- [ ] Every claim rests on an experience the applicant supplied
- [ ] The opening is specific to this applicant
- [ ] Fit names features of the programme and links them to past work
- [ ] No abstract virtue stands without evidence
- [ ] The integrity note is present

## Anti-Patterns

- **Do not invent experiences, results or motivations.**
- **Do not write in a voice far above the applicant's English level.**
- **Do not produce one essay for all programmes.**
- **Do not write the recommendation letter for the recommender to sign.**
- **Do not ignore an institution's rule against AI-written text.**

## Example Trigger Phrases

- "我申请英国的金融硕士，PS 没思路，帮我梳理素材。"
- "帮我看看这篇 SOP 哪里像模板。"
- "英国本科文书改成三个问题了，每个问题该写什么？"
- "Help me write a Why Us supplement for a US college from these notes."
