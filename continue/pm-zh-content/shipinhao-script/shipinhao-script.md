# 视频号脚本

WeChat Channels spreads through people, not only through an algorithm: a video travels when someone likes it and their friends see it, or when it is forwarded to a group. The audience skews older than on short-video apps and includes the creator's own contacts. That changes what works. Content that a person is glad to be seen sharing does better than content designed to shock. This skill writes for that setting and for the links into official accounts, enterprise WeChat and shops.

Write in Simplified Chinese.

## What This Skill Produces

- A hook for the first three seconds
- A timed script with spoken lines, visuals and captions
- A title with topics (话题) and an optional location
- A line that gives viewers a reason to share
- A call to action that follows platform rules
- A livestream run-of-show, if requested
- A compliance check

## Required Inputs

Ask for these if not provided:

- Account type: personal, business, institution, and the field
- Goal: reach, followers, leads into enterprise WeChat or a group, sales, bookings
- Topic and the single message
- Audience: age, relationship to the account (customers, peers, local residents)
- Format and length: short video, longer video, livestream
- Linked assets: official account (公众号), mini programme, shop, enterprise WeChat
- Products or services involved and any regulated category

## Framework

1. **Shareability first.** Ask: would a viewer forward this to a family or work group, and what would it say about them? Useful, warm, local and affirming content travels. Embarrassing or aggressive content does not.
2. **First three seconds.** A question the viewer has, a result, or a statement they want to agree with. Large captions, since many watch without sound.
3. **Language.** Spoken, plain, complete sentences. Avoid slang that the audience's parents would not understand.
4. **Length.** Most short videos work within a minute. One point per video.
5. **Structure.** Hook, the point with one example, what to do, share line.
6. **Title and topics.** A clear sentence, then two or three topics. Add a location for local businesses.
7. **Call to action.** Follow the account, read the linked article, visit the shop or mini programme through the official components. Follow platform rules on directing users to add contacts or leave the platform; use the provided links rather than on-screen QR codes or contact details where rules restrict them.
8. **Livestream.** Opening and welcome, value segment, product or service segments with a fixed rhythm, questions, closing and next session. Announce through the official account and groups beforehand.
9. **Compliance.** No exaggerated or absolute claims, no medical or financial promises, proper qualifications for regulated goods, and disclosure of advertising.

## Quality Checks

- [ ] The script states why a viewer would share it
- [ ] The hook lands within three seconds and has a caption
- [ ] The video makes one point
- [ ] The language suits an older and mixed audience
- [ ] The call to action uses official components
- [ ] Claims are free of absolute and regulated wording

## Anti-Patterns

- **Do not copy a Douyin script unchanged.**
- **Do not rely on sound.** Caption everything.
- **Do not put contact details or QR codes on screen** where platform rules restrict it.
- **Do not make the viewer look bad for sharing.**
- **Do not cram three topics into one video.**
