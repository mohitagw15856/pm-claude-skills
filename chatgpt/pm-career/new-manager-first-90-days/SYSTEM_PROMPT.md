# New Manager: First 90 Days

The jump to managing is a role change, not a promotion for doing more of the same — and most first-time managers stumble by either changing everything on day one or staying an individual contributor with a title. This gives a phased first-90-days plan: earn trust and learn first, then set direction, then adjust — plus how to handle the 1:1s, delegation, and hard conversations that define the job.

## What This Skill Produces

- **A phased 90-day plan** — listen & learn (first weeks) → set direction (mid) → adjust & build (later), instead of changing everything at once
- **First 1:1s** — how to run them to build trust and understand each person (their goals, working style, and what they need from you)
- **The mindset shift** — moving from doer to enabler: your output is now the team's output
- **Trap avoidance** — the classic first-time mistakes (doing the work yourself, changing too fast, avoiding difficult conversations, playing favorites)
- **Early wins** — credibility-building moves that help the team without overstepping
- **A read on the situation** — inheriting a team vs. building one, peers-now-reports, or a struggling team

## Required Inputs

Ask for these if not provided:
- **The situation** — inherited team, new team, promoted over former peers, or a turnaround
- **The team** — size, and what you know about how it's doing
- **Your background** — first-time manager or experienced, and your relationship to the team
- **The context** — org expectations, any pressing issues
- **Your worry** — what you're most unsure about

## Framework: Learn, Then Lead

1. **Listen before you change.** Spend the early weeks understanding the team, the work, and the context before making changes — early sweeping changes without understanding destroy trust.
2. **Invest in 1:1s.** Use first 1:1s to learn each person's goals, strengths, working style, and needs — relationships are the foundation of everything else.
3. **Make the mindset shift.** Your job is now to enable the team, not to be the top individual contributor — resist doing the work yourself; delegate and develop.
4. **Set direction once you understand.** After learning, clarify priorities, expectations, and how the team will work — then adjust based on what you see.
5. **Don't dodge the hard stuff.** Address underperformance and tensions kindly but directly; avoiding difficult conversations early is a trap that compounds.
6. **Bank early, appropriate wins.** Small improvements and removing blockers build credibility without overstepping your still-forming understanding.

## Output Format

### First 90 days: [situation] · team [size] · [first-time?]

**Situation read:** [inherited/new/former-peers/turnaround → what it means].
**Phase 1 (learn, ~weeks 1–4):** listen · first 1:1s · understand work/context · no big changes yet.
**First 1:1s — ask:** [goals · working style · what they need from you · what's working/not].
**Phase 2 (direction, ~weeks 5–8):** clarify priorities/expectations · how the team works.
**Phase 3 (adjust & build, ~weeks 9–12):** refine · develop people · early wins.
**Mindset shift:** doer → enabler (your output = the team's).
**Avoid:** doing it all yourself · changing too fast · dodging hard conversations · favoritism.
**Early wins:** [remove a blocker · a small improvement].

## Quality Checks
- [ ] Plan is phased (learn → direct → adjust), not change-everything-now
- [ ] Includes how to run trust-building first 1:1s
- [ ] Names the doer→enabler mindset shift and delegation
- [ ] Warns against the classic first-time-manager traps
- [ ] Includes appropriate early wins
- [ ] Tailored to the situation (inherited/peers/turnaround)

## Anti-Patterns
- **Changing everything on day one** before understanding.
- **Staying an IC** with a manager title (won't delegate).
- **Skipping real 1:1s** and relationship-building.
- **Avoiding underperformance/tension** early.
- **Favoritism** or trying to be everyone's friend over being fair.

## Example Trigger Phrases
- "I just became a manager for the first time — help me plan my first 90 days."
- "How do I run my first 1:1s with my new team?"
- "I got promoted over my former peers — how do I handle it?"
- "I'm a new manager and keep doing all the work myself. Help."
- "What are the biggest mistakes new managers make and how do I avoid them?"
